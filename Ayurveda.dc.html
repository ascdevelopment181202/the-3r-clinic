The three doshas

Every body is a unique blend of three energies.

Vata, Pitta and Kapha govern all of nature, and all of you. Balance is health; imbalance is where dis-ease begins.

Vata
Air + Ether

Movement and creativity, governs breath, circulation and the nervous system. Balanced: light, energetic, imaginative.

Pitta
Fire + Water

Transformation and focus, governs digestion, metabolism and intellect. Balanced: sharp, warm, driven.

Kapha
Earth + Water

Structure and calm, governs immunity, strength and stability. Balanced: grounded, loving, enduring.
